How to choose

Match your sleeping position

Side sleepers need softer pressure relief; back and stomach sleepers need firmer support.

Hot sleeper? Go hybrid

Coils let air flow. Look for cooling covers and gel or copper-infused foams.

Sharing a bed?

Memory foam isolates motion best; good edge support gives you more usable space.

Use the trial

Most boxed mattresses offer 90–365-night trials. Give your body 2–4 weeks to adjust.

Questions people ask

Foam or hybrid?

Foam hugs and isolates motion; hybrids add bounce, edge support and cooler sleep.

Which size do the links show?

The buttons open the queen size. You can switch sizes on Amazon.

When is the best time to buy?

Big sales around Black Friday and holiday weekends often bring the best prices.
