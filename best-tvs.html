How to choose

OLED or Mini-LED?

OLED gives perfect blacks and is best for movie nights in a dim room. Mini-LED gets brighter and resists glare, so it suits sunny living rooms and daytime sports.

Pick the right size

A simple rule: measure your seating distance in inches and divide by about 1.6. Sitting 8 feet (96") away? A 55–65 inch screen is a good fit.

Gamers: check the ports

Look for HDMI 2.1, 120Hz or higher refresh rate and VRR so a PS5, Xbox or gaming PC runs smoothly.

Plan for sound

Thin TVs have small speakers. Even a modest soundbar is a big upgrade for dialogue.

Questions people ask

What is the best TV overall in 2026?

The LG C6 OLED is the most common top pick across major review sites thanks to its balance of picture quality, gaming features and price.

Is last year's model worth buying?

Often, yes. Models like the LG C5 get big discounts once the new year's sets arrive, and the differences are usually small.

Do I need 8K?

No. There is very little 8K content, and 8K sets are becoming rare. A good 4K TV is the smart buy.
