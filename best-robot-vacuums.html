How to choose

Vacuum only, or vacuum + mop?

Combo robots save time on hard floors. If your home is mostly carpet, a strong vacuum-only robot is often enough.

Self-emptying is worth it

A self-emptying dock means you empty a bag every few weeks instead of a tiny bin every day.

Pets? Check the brush

Rubber or tangle-resistant brushes handle pet hair far better than bristle brushes.

Obstacle avoidance

Better models spot cables, socks and pet messes. Worth paying for if your floors are often cluttered.

Questions people ask

Do robot vacuums work on carpet?

Yes, but performance varies. Shark and Roborock models with strong suction do best on carpet.

How long do robot vacuums last?

Typically several years with regular brush and filter replacements.

Is Black Friday a good time to buy?

Yes — robot vacuums are among the most heavily discounted items during Black Friday sales.
