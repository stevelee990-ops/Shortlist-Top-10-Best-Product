How to choose

Check airline size limits

Most U.S. carry-on limits are about 22 x 14 x 9 inches including wheels and handles. International limits are often smaller.

Hardside or softside?

Hardside protects better and resists rain; softside flexes and usually has outside pockets.

Look at the warranty

Lifetime and 10-year warranties are a good sign the bag is built to last.

Weight matters

A lighter suitcase leaves more of your airline weight allowance for your stuff.

Questions people ask

What's the best carry-on size?

Look for about 22 x 14 x 9 inches for most U.S. airlines.

Is expensive luggage worth it?

Pricier bags usually roll smoother, last longer and come with better warranties.

Do I need a checked bag too?

For trips over a week or family travel, a large checked bag plus a carry-on is the most flexible combo.
