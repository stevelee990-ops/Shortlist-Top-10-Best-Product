How to choose

Listen for hints

The best gifts are often things she's mentioned wanting but wouldn't buy for herself.

Mix big and small

Pair a main gift with a small add-on like a candle or eye masks.

Comfort is always welcome

Sleep, self-care and cozy gifts are consistent favorites.

Questions people ask

What's a good gift for my wife?

Gifts that pamper her — a beauty tool, better sleep or a cozy blanket — are consistently popular.

What's a good gift under $50?

The Capri Blue Volcano candle or Grace & Stella eye masks are easy wins.

Is the Dyson Airwrap a good gift?
