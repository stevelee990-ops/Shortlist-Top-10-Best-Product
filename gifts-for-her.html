How to choose

Listen for hints

The best gifts are often things she's mentioned wanting but wouldn't buy for herself.

Mix big and small

Pair a main gift with a small add-on like a candle or eye masks.

Comfort is always welcome

Sleep, self-care and cozy gifts are consistent favorites.
