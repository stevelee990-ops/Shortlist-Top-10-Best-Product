How to choose

Foil or rotary?

Foil shavers (Braun, Panasonic) move in straight lines and usually shave closest. Rotary shavers (Philips Norelco) use circular heads that follow face contours — many find them more comfortable.

Match your skin and beard

Sensitive skin: try Braun or Philips' premium models. Thick, coarse beards: a fast multi-blade foil like the Panasonic Arc5.

Wet or dry

Wet & dry models let you shave with gel or in the shower, which many people find gentler.

Budget for heads

Plan to replace shaving heads or foils every 12–18 months to keep shaves close and comfortable.

Questions people ask

Do electric shavers shave as close as razors?

The best foil shavers get very close, though most people find a blade razor still slightly closer. The trade-off is far less irritation and nicks.

How long does it take to adjust?

Give your skin 2–3 weeks to adapt when switching from a blade razor.

Is a cleaning station worth it?

It's convenient and keeps the shaver hygienic, but cartridges add cost. Rinsing under water works fine for most wet & dry models.
