About us

The Shortlist makes buying decisions easier. Instead of reading a dozen long reviews, you get one clear, short list.

How we pick

For each category, we study the latest buying guides and hands-on reviews from respected independent testers. We look for products that show up again and again near the top, then group them by what they're best for — overall, budget, gaming, bright rooms and so on.

We write every summary in our own words and link our sources at the bottom of each list so you can read more. When we haven't tested a product ourselves, we say so — the picks reflect expert consensus, not made-up hands-on claims.
