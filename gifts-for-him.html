How to choose

Think about his routine

The best gifts improve something he does every day — coffee, commuting, workouts or weekend hobbies.

Upgrade what he already uses

A better version of something he loves (earbuds, shaver, wallet) is almost always a hit.

Experiences count too

Pair a gift like a pizza oven or fire pit with a planned night together.

Questions people ask

What do you get a man who has everything?

Upgrades and experiences work best: a premium version of something he uses daily, or a hobby gift like a pizza oven.
