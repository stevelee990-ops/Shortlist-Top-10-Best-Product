How to choose

Grinder in or out?

Fresh grinding matters a lot. A built-in grinder is simplest; machines without one need a separate espresso grinder, so budget for it.

How hands-on do you want to be?

Manual and semi-automatic machines reward practice. Super-automatic and capsule machines make coffee at the press of a button.

Lattes? Check the milk system

If you mostly drink milk drinks, look for automatic milk texturing or a strong steam wand.

Mind your counter

Measure your space. Compact machines like the Bambino fit small kitchens; all-in-ones can be big.

Questions people ask

What's the best espresso machine for beginners?

Machines with guidance and automation — like the Breville Barista Express Impress or De'Longhi La Specialista Touch — help you get good shots quickly.

Do I need a separate grinder?

Only if your machine doesn't have one built in. Pre-ground coffee works, but freshly ground beans taste noticeably better.

Is a capsule machine real espresso?

Not quite. Capsule machines like the VertuoPlus make espresso-style coffee, but not true pump espresso.
