How to choose

Dryer or multi-styler?

A good dryer is all most people need. Multi-stylers like the Airwrap replace several tools if you curl or smooth often.

Match your hair type

Curly hair loves a diffuser; fine hair does best with gentler, lighter dryers.

Weight matters

Lighter dryers (around 1 lb or less) are easier on your arms for long or thick hair.

Protect your hair

Use heat protectant and lower heat settings, especially with hot brushes.

Questions people ask

Is the Dyson Airwrap worth it?

If you style often and want one tool to dry and curl, many people think so. The Shark FlexStyle is a strong cheaper alternative.

Do expensive hair dryers really make a difference?

Pricier dryers are usually faster, lighter and quieter, and some protect your scalp from excess heat.

What's a hot brush?

A hot air brush dries and styles at the same time — great for quick, volumized blowouts.
