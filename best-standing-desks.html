How to choose

Check the height range

Make sure the desk goes low enough for sitting and high enough for standing at your height. Wider ranges suit more people.

Two motors and four legs = stability

Dual motors and four-leg frames wobble less, especially with big monitors.

Size your desktop

48 inches fits one monitor and a laptop. 55–60+ inches for two monitors.

Not ready for a full desk?

A desk converter turns your existing desk into a standing desk for much less.

Questions people ask

How long should I stand each day?

Alternating every 30–60 minutes is a common approach. Comfort matters more than a strict schedule.

Are cheap standing desks worth it?

Budget desks work well for light setups, but expect more wobble at full height.

Do I need an anti-fatigue mat?

It helps a lot if you stand for long periods.
