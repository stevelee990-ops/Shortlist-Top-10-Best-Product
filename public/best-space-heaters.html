How to choose

Size it to your room

Plan on about 10 watts per square foot. A 1,500-watt heater, the most a standard household outlet can supply, covers roughly 150 sq ft. High ceilings, drafty windows and poor insulation call for more heat, so err on the powerful side and turn it down.

Pick the right type

Ceramic fan heaters warm quickly and cost less but are louder and can make air feel dry. Oil-filled radiators are slow but nearly silent and hold steady heat. Infrared heaters warm the people and objects in front of them and run quietly.

Insist on automatic shutoff

Choose a heater that turns itself off if it tips over or overheats, carries a UL, ETL or CSA safety mark, and has a cord at least 6 ft long.

Plug it in safely

Plug straight into a wall outlet, never an extension cord or power strip. Set it on a hard, level surface (not carpet), at least 3 ft from curtains, bedding and furniture.

Extras that save energy

A thermostat or eco mode cycles the heater off once the room is warm. A timer, remote and oscillation make it easier to heat only when and where you need it.

Questions people ask

What is the safest space heater?

The safest space heaters shut off automatically if they tip over or overheat and carry a UL, ETL or CSA mark. Tom's Guide names the Dreo Whole Room Heater 714 the safest it tested, adding a child lock and a cool-touch, flame-retardant housing to tip-over and overheat protection.

What is the most energy-efficient space heater?

Bob Vila's top energy-efficient pick is the Dr. Infrared Heater DR-968. It notes that infrared heaters use up to 10% less energy than coil-and-fan models and that oil-filled radiators are among the most efficient; space heaters aren't Energy Star rated, so a thermostat or eco mode is the best way to save power.

Can you leave a space heater on overnight?

No. HGTV and Tom's Guide both say not to run a space heater while you sleep: warm the room, then turn the heater off before bed. Bob Vila notes the exception is an oil-filled radiator whose manual says overnight use is allowed.

What size space heater do I need?

Allow about 10 watts per square foot: a 10 x 12 ft room (120 sq ft) needs about 1,200 watts, and a 1,500-watt heater covers about 150 sq ft. Ceiling height, windows and insulation change the math.

Is an oil-filled or ceramic space heater better?

Ceramic heaters warm a room faster and cost less but are louder and can dry the air; oil-filled radiators are nearly silent and give steady heat but are slow (Bob Vila's top oil-filled pick took about 2 hours to warm a living room 10°F). Choose ceramic for quick spot heat and oil-filled for quiet, all-evening warmth.

Can you plug a space heater into an extension cord?

No. Bob Vila and Tom's Guide both say to plug a space heater directly into a wall outlet and never use an extension cord or power strip, and to keep it 3 ft away from anything that can burn.
