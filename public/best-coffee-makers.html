How to choose

Know what a 'cup' means

Coffee makers count 5-oz cups, so a 12-cup machine makes about seven 8-oz mugs and a 5-cup machine about two 12-oz mugs. One or two drinkers are fine with 5–8 cups; households and offices want 10–14.

Look for SCA certification

The Specialty Coffee Association certifies home brewers that hit its targets for water temperature (about 195–205°F), brew time (roughly 4–8 minutes) and even saturation. Testers at Food Network and HGTV found SCA-certified machines tended to taste better.

Thermal or glass carafe?

A glass carafe costs less and lets you see how much is left, but it sits on a hot plate that can make coffee taste scorched over time. A double-walled thermal carafe keeps coffee hot for hours without a hot plate, though it's harder to clean.

Programmable or not?

A programmable start (up to 24 hours ahead on the Cuisinart PerfecTemp) means coffee is ready when you wake up. Some of the best-tasting brewers, like the Moccamaster KBGV Select and OXO Brew 8-Cup, skip timers entirely, so decide which matters more.

Measure your counter and cabinets

Check the height under your upper cabinets before you buy: HGTV notes tall machines such as the Cuisinart PerfecTemp and Café Specialty Drip may not clear them. Removable water tanks and dishwasher-safe parts make daily use easier.

Questions people ask

What is the best coffee maker in 2026?

The Technivorm Moccamaster KBGV Select is the best coffee maker for most coffee lovers in 2026: it's the top drip pick at Tom's Guide and Reviewed, and Food Network testers rated its coffee highest. On a budget, the BLACK+DECKER 12-Cup Programmable is Food Network's best basic pick.

Is a Moccamaster worth it?

Yes, if you drink drip coffee every day and care about flavor: the KBGV Select is SCA-certified, heats water to 196–205°F in under a minute, brews a full pot in about 4 to 6 minutes and carries a 5-year warranty. It isn't programmable, so pick something like the Cuisinart PerfecTemp if you want a timer.

What does SCA certified mean on a coffee maker?

It means the Specialty Coffee Association has tested the brewer and confirmed it meets its standards for water temperature, brew time and how evenly the grounds are soaked. Picks here with SCA certification include the Moccamaster KBGV Select, OXO Brew 8-Cup, Breville Precision Brewer and Café Specialty Drip.

Is a thermal carafe better than glass?

For flavor, usually yes: a thermal carafe keeps coffee hot without a warming plate, while a glass carafe's hot plate can scorch coffee the longer it sits. Glass carafes are cheaper and easier to clean, and thermal carafes are harder to see into and to scrub.

How often should you clean a coffee maker?

Rinse or wash the carafe and brew basket after every use, and descale the machine every one to three months depending on how hard your water is. Use the self-clean cycle if it has one, or a vinegar-and-water mix or descaling solution, following the manufacturer's instructions.

What is the best coffee maker with a grinder?

The Café Specialty Grind and Brew is Reviewed's best coffee maker with a grinder; it grinds whole beans before brewing and makes anything from a single cup to a 10-cup carafe. Reviewed notes a built-in grinder adds cost and cleaning, so a separate burr grinder can be the better long-term setup.
