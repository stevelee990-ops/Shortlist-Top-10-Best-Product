How to choose

Decide how much noise cancelling you need

If you fly or commute, the Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen) and Sony WH-1000XM6 lead for active noise cancelling (ANC). Sound-first pairs like the Sennheiser HDB 630 cancel less noise, and wired pairs like the Røde NTH-100 rely only on closed-back ear cups.

Compare battery life with ANC on

Rated battery ranges from 20 hours (AirPods Max 2) to 30 hours (Sony WH-1000XM6, Bose QC Ultra 2nd Gen), 60 hours (Sennheiser HDB 630) and about 75 hours with ANC on for the Nothing Headphone (a). Check the ANC-on number, since that's how most people listen.

Weight affects comfort

Over-ear headphones in this list run from about 7.3 oz (Sony WH-CH730N, 207 g) to about 13.6 oz (AirPods Max 2). Heavier pairs press more on the head over long flights; reviewers call the AirPods Max 2 and Nothing Headphone (a) heavy.

Match your phone

AirPods Max 2 work best with iPhone, iPad and Mac and have no companion app. NBC Select notes the other top picks work equally well with iPhone and Android, so they're the safer choice for mixed households.

Closed-back or open-back?

Every pick here is closed-back, which keeps sound in and outside noise out: right for travel, offices and shared rooms. Open-back headphones sound airier but leak sound, so they're best kept for quiet listening at home.

Check the case and wired options

The Sony, Bose and Soundcore pairs come with hard cases; AirPods Max 2 ship with a soft cover and the Nothing Headphone (a) with no case. The Bose and Sennheiser can play audio over USB-C, but the HDB 630 needs charge even when wired, and the Soundcore Life Q30 can't use ANC over a cable.

Questions people ask

What are the best over-ear headphones in 2026?

The Sony WH-1000XM6 are the best over-ear headphones for most people in 2026: they're the top pick at NBC Select, TechRadar and Engadget, with class-leading noise cancelling, detailed sound and 30 hours of battery with ANC on. The Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones (2nd Gen) are the best alternative if blocking noise matters most.

Sony WH-1000XM6 or Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen): which is better?

The Sony WH-1000XM6 is the better all-rounder, while the Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen) has stronger noise cancelling. After six months with both, Tom's Guide picked the Sony for its 10-band EQ (Bose offers 3 bands), better spatial audio and simpler design, but found the Bose blocks more noise.

Are the AirPods Max 2 worth it?

The AirPods Max 2 are worth it mainly for iPhone owners who want seamless Apple features with great sound and noise cancelling. For everyone else they're hard to justify: they list at $549, weigh about 13.6 oz, last about 20 hours with ANC on and come with a soft cover instead of a hard case.

What are the best budget over-ear headphones?

The Soundcore Life Q30 are the best budget over-ear headphones, NBC Select's pick, with noise cancelling, a hard case and 40 hours of battery with ANC on for well under $100. For a step up under $200, the Sony WH-CH730N won a What Hi-Fi? Award for best cheap noise-cancelling headphones.

Which over-ear headphones have the longest battery life?

The Nothing Headphone (a) have the longest battery life here: up to 135 hours with noise cancelling off and about 75 hours with it on, per TechRadar. The Sennheiser HDB 630 last about 60 hours and the Sony WH-CH730N up to 50 hours with ANC on.

Are noise-cancelling headphones bad for your ears?

No, noise-cancelling headphones are not bad for your ears. TechRadar notes that ANC can actually help protect your hearing, because with less background noise you can listen at a lower volume.
