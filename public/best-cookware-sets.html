How to choose

Stainless or nonstick?

Stainless lasts forever and browns food best. Nonstick and ceramic make eggs and cleanup easy. Many cooks own both.

Induction cooktop?

Check that the set is induction-ready — not all aluminum or ceramic sets are.

Fewer, better pieces

A 10-piece set with the sizes you'll use beats a giant set full of extras.

Care for nonstick

Use medium heat and wooden or silicone utensils to make nonstick coatings last.

Questions people ask

What's the best cookware material?

Tri-ply stainless steel is the most versatile and long-lasting; nonstick is easiest for everyday eggs and fish.

Is ceramic cookware safe?

Ceramic coatings are marketed as PFAS-free, which is why many people choose them.

How long does nonstick last?

Typically a few years with care. Stainless steel can last a lifetime.
