How to choose

Match the IP rating to where you'll use it

The first digit is dust protection (up to 6) and the second is water (up to 8); an X means it wasn't tested. IP67 and IP68 speakers such as the JBL Charge 6 and Wonderboom 4 survive a dunk, while the Marshall Kilburn III only handles splashes. If it may end up in a pool, get one that floats, like the Wonderboom 4.

Treat battery claims as a best case

Engadget notes that rated hours are usually measured at low to mid volume, and loud playback can cut them by half or more. About 12 hours is a sensible minimum; the Charge 6 (24 hours) and Kilburn III (50 hours) are the picks for weekends away.

Pick the size for the job

Palm-sized speakers like the JBL Go 5 and Tribit PocketGo (under 8 oz) clip to a bag or shower rod; bottle-sized Flip 7 and Wonderboom 4 (about 1 lb) fill a patio; party speakers like the JBL Xtreme 5 (about 6.4 lbs) carry a backyard gathering.

Don't shop by watts

Tom's Guide and Popular Science both warn that wattage and frequency specs aren't comparable between brands. Use reviews to judge sound, and remember a 4.8W Go 5 is for one room while the 90W Xtreme 5 is for a crowd.

Check pairing before you mix brands

JBL's newer speakers (Go 5, Flip 7, Charge 6, Xtreme 5) use Auracast and won't link with older PartyBoost JBLs, per What Hi-Fi?. Auracast speakers can link with other Auracast models, and Ultimate Ears and Tribit pair within their own lines.

Want Wi-Fi at home? Buy it once

If you'd also use it as a home speaker, a Wi-Fi model such as the Sonos Play streams in higher quality, groups with other Sonos speakers and has voice control, then switches to Bluetooth outdoors.

Questions people ask

What is the best portable Bluetooth speaker in 2026?

The JBL Charge 6 is the best portable Bluetooth speaker for most people: it's What Hi-Fi?'s best overall and a 2026 Awards winner, with IP68 waterproofing, up to 24 hours of battery (28 with Playtime Boost) and a built-in power bank. Tom's Guide's top pick is the Marshall Kilburn III, which trades waterproofing for 50 hours of battery.

What is the best Bluetooth speaker under $50?

The JBL Go 5 is the best Bluetooth speaker under $50, at about $40 on Amazon when What Hi-Fi? (its best budget pick) checked. For even less, Tom's Guide's best cheap pick, the Tribit PocketGo, costs under $40 and is rated for 20 hours.

What is the best waterproof Bluetooth speaker?

The Ultimate Ears Wonderboom 4 is the best waterproof Bluetooth speaker for the pool or beach because it's IP67 rated and floats; NBC Select notes most rivals sink. For dust and water protection on land, the JBL Charge 6, Flip 7, Go 5 and Xtreme 5 are all rated IP68.

Which is better, the JBL Flip 7 or the Charge 6?

The JBL Charge 6 is better if you want more volume, bass and battery (24 vs 14 hours) plus a power bank; the Flip 7 is better if you want a lighter speaker (about 1.2 vs 2.1 lbs) for about $40 less. What Hi-Fi? gives both five stars and says the choice comes down to budget and how you'll use it.

What does IP67 mean on a speaker?

IP67 means the speaker is dust-tight (6) and can survive being submerged in water temporarily (7), typically up to about 1 meter for 30 minutes. IP68 offers longer or deeper submersion protection, while IPX4 only covers splashes, as Tom's Guide notes for the Sony ULT Tower 5.

Can you connect two Bluetooth speakers together?

Yes, most speakers on this list can pair with a second matching unit for stereo or bigger sound. Compatibility depends on the brand: newer JBL models link over Auracast but not with older JBLs, and What Hi-Fi? says Auracast speakers can link with other Auracast models, such as a JBL Charge 6 with a Harman Kardon Luna 2.
