How to choose

Pick the right size

2–4 quarts suits one or two people. 5–6 quarts feeds a family of four. 8+ quarts or dual baskets for bigger households.

Basket or oven-style?

Basket air fryers crisp best. Oven-style models do more (toast, bake, roast) but take more space.

Look for easy cleaning

Dishwasher-safe baskets and trays save a lot of hassle.

Measure your counter

Some air fryers are tall or wide. Check height under your cabinets.

Questions people ask

What size air fryer do I need?

For most households of 3–4 people, a 5–6 quart model is the sweet spot.

Are dual-basket air fryers worth it?

Yes, if you often cook a main and a side together — both finish at the same time.

Can an air fryer replace my oven?

An oven-style air fryer can for many meals. Basket models are best for crispy foods and reheating.
