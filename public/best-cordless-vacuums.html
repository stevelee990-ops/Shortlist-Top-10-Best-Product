How to choose

Check real runtime, not the box

Rated runtimes such as 60 or 70 minutes are measured in eco mode. On max or Turbo, TechGearLab measured as little as 7 to 16 minutes, so for a whole-house clean look for at least 40 minutes on standard power or a swappable battery.

Weight matters more than you think

Most stick vacuums weigh about 5 to 9 pounds. Lighter models like the Tineco Pure ONE S11 (about 5.7 lb) are easier on stairs and overhead; heavier ones like the Shark PowerDetect (about 8.4 to 8.8 lb) tend to clean deeper but tire your arm sooner.

Trigger or button?

Dyson V-series models make you hold a trigger the whole time, which testers at Forbes Vetted and TechGearLab found tiring. The Levoit LVAC-300, Tineco Station 5 and Dyson Gen5detect use a one-press power button.

Pet hair needs the right head

For pet hair, look for an anti-tangle or comb-style brush roll, strong suction and a motorized mini tool for furniture and stairs, like the Bissell FurFinder. A bin of at least 0.5 liters saves trips to the trash.

Self-emptying docks save a messy job

Docks on the Shark PowerDetect Clean & Empty and Tineco Station 5 empty the bin for you, which helps with allergies and pet hair, but they need floor space and cost more.

Keep it clean

Empty the bin often, rinse washable filters and let them dry fully, and cut hair off the brush roll. Engadget also suggests wiping sensors and charging contacts so the vacuum keeps running at full power.

Questions people ask

What is the best cordless vacuum in 2026?

The Dyson V15 Detect is the best cordless vacuum for most people in 2026: it's the top pick at RTINGS, Engadget and HGTV, with strong suction on every floor type and up to 60 minutes of runtime. Forbes Vetted's top pick, the Shark PowerDetect Clean & Empty, is the best alternative if you want a self-emptying dock.

Is a Dyson cordless vacuum worth it?

A Dyson is worth it if you want top cleaning and pet hair performance; the V15 Detect and Gen5detect lead several 2026 tests. But Dysons cost the most, and Shark, Levoit, Tineco and Dreame models clean well for far less: TechRadar's best overall, the Dreame R20, costs about $150.

How long do cordless vacuum batteries last on a charge?

Most cordless vacuums run about 30 to 40 minutes per charge, and some reach 60 to 70 minutes, Engadget says. Those numbers are for the lowest power setting; on max power TechGearLab measured only about 7 to 16 minutes.

Can a cordless vacuum replace a regular vacuum?

Yes, a good cordless vacuum can replace a corded one in many homes, but in larger homes it often works best as a second vacuum for quick cleanups. Cordless models trade some suction and runtime for convenience, though pricier models come close to corded power.

What is the best cordless vacuum for pet hair?

The Bissell PowerClean FurFinder is Forbes Vetted's best cordless vacuum for pet hair, and the Dyson V15 Detect is outstanding on pet hair in RTINGS' tests. Look for strong suction, an anti-tangle brush roll and a motorized mini tool for furniture.

Shark or Dyson: which cordless vacuum is better?

Dyson usually wins on light weight, dust detection and premium features, while Shark offers self-emptying docks and strong cleaning for less money. In 2026 tests, the Dyson V15 Detect is RTINGS' and Engadget's top pick, and the Shark PowerDetect is Forbes Vetted's and TechGearLab's.
