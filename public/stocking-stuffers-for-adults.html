How to choose

Aim for useful things they'll use every week

The stocking stuffers editors repeat most solve small daily problems: a tracker for keys (AirTag), a charger that replaces a cable and wall block (Anker Nano 3-in-1), or good socks.

Check their phone before buying tech

The AirTag (2nd Generation) needs an iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or later, and its Precision Finding needs an iPhone 15 or newer. For a USB-C power bank, make sure their phone charges over USB-C.

Think small enough to fit

A standard stocking holds items about the size of a speaker like the JBL Go 4 or a 6 oz jar of chili crisp; save bigger gifts like the Theragun Mini Plus for under the tree or the top of the stocking.

Mix one 'real' gift with consumables

A good stocking for an adult pairs one gadget (an AirFly SE for a frequent flyer) with things that get used up, like shower steamers, a lip mask or a hot sauce — so nothing feels like clutter.

Budget about $10–$50 per item

Most picks here cost under $50, from about $10 for Fly By Jing chili crisp to about $40 for the AirFly SE or JBL Go 4.
