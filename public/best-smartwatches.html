How to choose

Start with your phone

An Apple Watch works only with an iPhone. The Pixel Watch 5, Galaxy Watch 9 and OnePlus Watch 3 need an Android phone, and the Galaxy Watch 9's ECG and blood pressure features need a Samsung phone. Garmin, Amazfit and Withings watches work with both.

Smartwatch or fitness watch?

Apple, Google and Samsung watches have the best apps, payments, voice assistants and replies, but last about 18–48 hours. Fitness-first watches like the Garmin Venu 4 (up to 12 days) and Forerunner 165 (up to 11 days) trade some smart features for much longer battery and deeper training data.

Compare battery in hours vs. days

Rated battery ranges from 18 hours (Apple Watch SE 3) and 24 hours (Series 12) to 30–40 hours (Pixel Watch 5, Galaxy Watch 9), 120 hours (OnePlus Watch 3) and up to about 35 days (Withings ScanWatch 2). Turning on an always-on display cuts these numbers.

Pick the right case size

Most watches come in a small and large size: 40/44mm (SE 3, Galaxy Watch 9), 41/45mm (Pixel Watch 5, Venu 4) or 42/46mm (Series 12). The larger size usually has a bigger battery, like the Pixel Watch 5's 40 hours vs. 30 for the 41mm.

Check which health features you'll get

Sleep apnea detection is on the Apple Watch Series 12 and Galaxy Watch 9, while the Pixel Watch 5 adds Loss of Pulse Detection. The Apple Watch SE 3 skips ECG, blood oxygen and sleep apnea features, so pay up if those matter.

Decide on cellular (LTE)

GPS/Bluetooth models need your phone nearby for calls and data. LTE versions cost about $50–$100 more (for example $429 vs. $379 for the Galaxy Watch 9 40mm) plus a monthly carrier fee.

Questions people ask

What is the best smartwatch in 2026?

The Apple Watch Series 12 is the best smartwatch in 2026 for iPhone owners: Engadget and TechRadar both rank it best overall, with a new S11 chip, a free daily Readiness score, hypertension alerts and up to 24 hours of battery. Android users should get the Google Pixel Watch 5, Engadget's best smartwatch for Android.

What is the best smartwatch for Android?

The Google Pixel Watch 5 is the best smartwatch for most Android phones, with Loss of Pulse Detection, better GPS and 30–40 hours of rated battery. If you have a Samsung phone, the Samsung Galaxy Watch 9 is TechRadar's best for health tracking and unlocks ECG and blood pressure features.

Can you use an Apple Watch with an Android phone?

No, an Apple Watch only works with an iPhone. Pixel and Galaxy watches need an Android phone, while Garmin, Amazfit and Withings watches work with both iPhone and Android.

What is the best cheap smartwatch?

The Amazfit Active 3 Premium ($169.99) is Tom's Guide's best budget smartwatch, with about eight days of battery in testing and no subscription for core features. Under $100, the Amazfit Active 2 ($99) is TechRadar's best cheap watch; for iPhone, the Apple Watch SE 3 starts at $249.

Which smartwatch has the longest battery life?

The Withings ScanWatch 2 lasts the longest here, up to about 35 days, because it's a hybrid with analog hands. Among touchscreen watches, the Garmin Venu 4 (up to 12 days) and Garmin Forerunner 165 (up to 11 days) last longest, and the OnePlus Watch 3 (up to 120 hours) is the longest-lasting Wear OS watch.

Is a Garmin better than an Apple Watch?

A Garmin is better for training and battery life, while an Apple Watch is better for smart features on iPhone. The Garmin Venu 4 lasts up to 12 days and has Garmin's full training tools, compared with about 24 hours for the Apple Watch Series 12, but Apple has far more apps, replies and payment support.
