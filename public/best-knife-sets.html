How to choose

Start with the big three

A chef's knife, a bread knife and a paring knife cover most cooking, according to America's Test Kitchen. Aim for an 8-inch chef's knife, a bread knife of at least 10 inches for big loaves, and a 3- to 4-inch paring knife; Food Network adds a utility knife as the third essential.

Don't pay for pieces you won't use

Big sets can pad the piece count with steak knives, extra paring knives, blocks and shears. ATK warns that mediocre extras inflate the price, and sets aren't always cheaper: the Mercer Culinary Millennia 3-Piece cost more than its knives bought separately.

Forged or stamped?

Forged blades, like the Wüsthof Classic, are sturdier, hold an edge longer and usually have a bolster and full tang, but they're heavier and cost more. Stamped blades are lighter and easier to maneuver, and usually cheaper.

Western or Japanese style?

German and other Western knives are thicker and heavier, good for squash and chicken. Japanese knives such as the Shun Classic are thinner, harder and sharper but need more careful handling; Food Network says the Shun Classic isn't suited to crusty bread or bone-heavy jobs.

Pick storage for your counter

A block protects the blades but takes counter space. A magnetic stand (Material), magnetic wall strip or in-drawer tray (Shun Premier) saves room; never store knives loose in a drawer without blade guards.

Plan to hone and sharpen

Hand wash and dry knives right away; Reviewed and CNN Underscored both advise against the dishwasher. Hone often, and resharpen about once a year (Food Network) or whenever a blade no longer slices cleanly through paper or tomato skin.

Questions people ask

What is the best knife set in 2026?

The Wüsthof Classic 9-Piece Knife Block Set is the best knife set for most people in 2026: it's Food Network's best overall pick, with forged, well-balanced knives for every job. For much less money, America's Test Kitchen's winner is the Victorinox Fibrox 4-Piece Knife Set.

Is it better to buy a knife set or individual knives?

Buying individual knives is better if you want the best knife for each job or you're still learning what you need, America's Test Kitchen says. A set usually costs less than its knives bought separately (savings of up to about 38% in ATK's lineup) but not always, so compare before you buy.

What knives should a good knife set include?

A good knife set should include a chef's knife, a serrated bread knife and a paring or utility knife. A honing steel and kitchen shears are useful extras; boning, carving and steak knives are optional.

Are self-sharpening knife blocks worth it?

A self-sharpening block is worth it if you don't want to hone knives yourself: in sets like the Henckels Classic Precision 15-Piece, each slot hones the blade every time you remove or return the knife. You'll still need a full sharpening now and then.

Are forged knives better than stamped?

Forged knives are sturdier and hold an edge longer, but stamped knives are lighter, easier to maneuver and usually cheaper. Forged sets like the Wüsthof Classic usually have a bolster and full tang, which add weight and cost, Food Network notes.

Can you put a knife set in the dishwasher?

No, most knife sets should be washed by hand and dried right away. Reviewed and CNN Underscored both advise hand washing to protect the edges and finish.
