How to choose

Check which console they own

Controllers, headsets and games are often platform-specific: the DualSense Edge is for PS5, the Turtle Beach Stealth Pro II comes in Xbox and PlayStation/PC versions, and KontrolFreek sticks come in PlayStation and Xbox versions.

Match the gift to how they play

PC gamers get the most from a Steam Deck OLED or a Logitech G502 HERO; couch and family players from a Nintendo Switch 2; anyone curious about VR from a Meta Quest 3S, which needs no PC or console.

For the gamer who has everything, go retro or collectible

Nostalgic gifts like the LEGO Game Boy (421 pieces) or the Evercade Nexus with Banjo-Kazooie are things most players don't buy for themselves.

Under $50, upgrade what they already use

Thumbstick grips such as the KontrolFreek FPS Freek Galaxy (about $17) or a game-themed cookbook make easy stocking stuffers; a Steam, PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo gift card is the safe choice if you're unsure.

Questions people ask

What is the best gift for a gamer in 2026?

The Nintendo Switch 2 is the best all-round gamer gift in 2026: it plays on a TV or as a handheld, and Tom's Guide highlights the Mario Kart World Bundle, which includes the game and three months of Nintendo Switch Online.

What do you get a gamer who has everything?

Get something they wouldn't buy themselves, like the LEGO Game Boy display set, the Evercade Nexus retro handheld or a pro controller such as the DualSense Edge. A gift card for their platform is the fallback when you're not sure what they own.

What are good gifts for gamers under $50?

The KontrolFreek FPS Freek Galaxy thumbsticks (about $17) and The Elder Scrolls: The Official Cookbook are both under $50, and the Logitech G502 HERO mouse is often on sale near $40. Steam and console gift cards come in any amount.

What should I get a PC gamer?

A Steam Deck OLED lets a PC gamer play their Steam library anywhere, with more than 22,000 games rated Verified or Playable. For their desk, the Logitech G502 HERO mouse (11 programmable buttons) is a Which? members' favorite.

Is the Meta Quest 3S a good gift?

Yes, for anyone curious about VR: Tom's Guide calls it one of the best VR headsets for gamers, and it works on its own with no PC or console. The trade-offs versus the Quest 3 are lower-resolution screens and a narrower field of view.

Is the PS5 DualSense Edge worth it?

It's worth it for serious PS5 players who want back buttons and adjustable triggers. It costs about $199 and includes swappable stick caps, two kinds of back buttons, a carrying case and a locking braided cable.
