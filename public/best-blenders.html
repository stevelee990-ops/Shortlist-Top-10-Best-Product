How to choose

Pick the right size

Single-serve blenders (about 16–26 oz) suit one or two drinks you take with you. Full-size jugs of 48–72 oz are better for families, batch smoothies, soups and nut butter; the Ninja BL610's 72 oz pitcher is the largest pick here.

Wattage is a guide, not a guarantee

HGTV suggests about 1,000 watts or more for easy, reliable blending, but Food Network and Tom's Guide note wattage alone doesn't measure performance: motor design and container size matter too. A smaller single-serve blender can do well with less power than a big jug.

Check before blending hot soup

Only blend hot ingredients in a blender made for it, such as the Vitamix Explorian E310, and use a vented lid so pressure can't build up. HGTV says the Ninja BL610 isn't suited to hot soups.

Plastic or glass?

Most high-speed blenders use BPA-free Tritan plastic because glass could shatter at those speeds. If you'd rather avoid plastic, the Oster Master Series uses a shatter-resistant glass jar; glass jars are heavier.

Load it the right way

Put liquids and tough greens like kale at the bottom, then frozen and hard ingredients on top. Don't run every task on the top speed: Food Network notes that loading incorrectly or always blending on high can strain the motor.

Make cleanup easy

Look for dishwasher-safe parts or removable blades (both Ninja jug picks have them). The quickest clean: add a little dish soap and warm water and run the blender on high for 30–60 seconds, then rinse.

Questions people ask

What is the best blender in 2026?

The Vitamix Explorian E310 is the best blender for most people in 2026: it's Food Network's best overall after testing more than 21 blenders and the best smoothie blender RTINGS has tested. On a budget, the Ninja Professional Blender (BL610) is Food Network's and HGTV's best value pick at around $80.

Is a Vitamix worth it?

A Vitamix is worth it if you blend often and want one machine that handles smoothies, nut butter and hot soups for years: the Explorian E310 has a 1,400-watt motor and handles hot liquids, and the Ascent X2 carries a 10-year warranty. If you mostly make smoothies, the Ninja Detect Power Blender Pro came close to Vitamix performance in Tom's Guide's tests for much less.

What is the best blender for smoothies?

The Vitamix Explorian E310 is RTINGS' best blender for smoothies, and the Ninja Professional Blender (BL610) is Food Network's best overall smoothie blender for the price. For single servings you drink from the cup, TechRadar's pick is the Ninja BlendBoss.

How many watts should a good blender have?

Aim for about 1,000 watts or more for a full-size blender that crushes ice and frozen fruit easily, HGTV advises. Tom's Guide notes a single-serve blender can work well with less, around 900 watts, because wattage depends on motor type and container size.

Can you put hot liquids in a blender?

Yes, but only in a blender designed for it and with a vented lid, because steam can build pressure and push the lid off. The Vitamix Explorian E310 is made to handle hot liquids, while HGTV says the Ninja BL610 isn't suited to hot soups.

How do you clean a blender?

Add a couple of drops of dish soap and some warm water, run the blender on high for 30 seconds to a minute, then drain and rinse. For stuck-on residue, TechRadar suggests a paste of one tablespoon of baking soda and one teaspoon of white vinegar, scrubbed with a brush.
