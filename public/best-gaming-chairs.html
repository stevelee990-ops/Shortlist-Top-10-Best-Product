How to choose

Pick the right size first

Check the height and weight range before anything else. Secretlab sizes the Titan Evo Small for people up to 5'6" and 200 lb, Regular for 5'7"–6'2" and under 220 lb, and XL for 5'11"–6'9" and up to 395 lb. TechRadar suggests an XL model if you're taller than 6'2" or heavier than 200 lb, and Tom's Hardware says to check both ends of the range if several people will share the chair.

Get real lumbar support

PC Gamer calls lumbar support a must and a headrest optional. Built-in adjustable lumbar (4-way on the Secretlab Titan Evo and AndaSeat Kaiser 3 XL, 6D on the Razer Iskur V2) fits more backs than a strap-on pillow like the Corsair TC100 Relaxed's.

Choose fabric, mesh or leatherette

Mesh and fabric stay cooler in long sessions; TechRadar says they're also more comfortable over time than leatherette but harder to keep free of pet hair. If you're prone to spills, PC Gamer suggests leatherette or mesh, which wipe clean more easily than fabric.

Know your armrests and recline

4D armrests move up/down, forward/back, side to side and rotate; budget chairs like the Razer Iskur V2 X have 2D arms. Racing-style chairs recline to 152–165°, while office-style gaming chairs like the Herman Miller x Logitech G Vantum and Embody only tilt. Tom's Hardware notes many 4D arms don't lock by design; if they wobble at the base, tighten the bolts underneath.

Budget about $300–$700

Tom's Guide calls $300–$700 the sweet spot. Budget chairs start around $200, racing-style chairs from Secretlab and AndaSeat run about $450–$650, and Herman Miller office-style chairs cost much more but carry 12-year warranties versus the 2–5 years typical of gaming chairs (Tom's Hardware). TechRadar suggests waiting for sales like Black Friday.

Set it up properly

PC Gamer's posture checklist: knees at about 90°, feet flat on the floor, elbows level with the desk, about two inches between the seat edge and the back of your knees, and the top of your monitor at eye level. Stand up or move every 30–60 minutes.

Questions people ask

What is the best gaming chair in 2026?

The Secretlab Titan Evo is the best gaming chair in 2026 for most people: PC Gamer, Tom's Guide and TechRadar all rank it best overall. It comes in three sizes (XL holds up to 395 lb), has a 4-way adjustable built-in lumbar, a magnetic head pillow and a 165° recline, and costs about $549 for the regular model.

Are gaming chairs good for your back?

A gaming chair can be good for your back if it fits your size and has adjustable lumbar support. PC Gamer says lumbar support is a must and that adjustability and movement matter most. Tom's Guide's pick for back pain is the Razer Iskur V2, whose lumbar adjusts in six directions; whatever you sit in, get up every 30–60 minutes.

Is a gaming chair or an office chair better?

Neither is better for everyone: gaming chairs usually have taller backrests, headrests, more padding and deeper recline, while office chairs look more subdued and often have longer warranties (12 years for Herman Miller vs. 2–5 years for most gaming chairs, per Tom's Hardware). If you work and game at the same desk, a gaming-focused office chair like the Herman Miller x Logitech G Vantum is a good middle ground.

How much should I spend on a gaming chair?

Plan on about $300–$700, the sweet spot according to Tom's Guide. Good budget chairs like the Corsair TC100 Relaxed and Razer Iskur V2 X cost about $200–$300, racing-style chairs from Secretlab and AndaSeat run about $450–$650, and premium ergonomic chairs like the LiberNovo Omni and Herman Miller x Logitech G Embody cost $1,000 or more.

What size Secretlab Titan Evo should I get?

Get the Small if you're up to 5'6" and under 200 lb, the Regular if you're 5'7"–6'2" and under 220 lb, and the XL if you're 5'11"–6'9" and up to 395 lb, according to Secretlab's sizing guide. If you fall between sizes, compare weight as well as height.

How long does a gaming chair last?

A good gaming chair should last at least two to three years of daily use, according to Tom's Guide, and better ones last much longer: PC Gamer's reviewer has used a Secretlab Titan Evo since 2021 and it's still in excellent condition. Warranties are a good guide, from 2 years on budget chairs to 12 years on Herman Miller models.
