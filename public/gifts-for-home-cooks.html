How to choose

Upgrade what they use every day

Cooks love better versions of daily tools: a 5.5–6.5-quart enameled Dutch oven, an 8-inch chef's knife or a scale that weighs up to 11 lb (5 kg) all get used weekly.

Thermometers are the easiest win

A wireless probe like the CHEF iQ Sense alerts their phone at the target temperature — ideal for holiday turkey and prime rib, and usually under $50.

Match the gift to their style

Bakers: scale, vanilla bean paste. Meat lovers: thermometer, sous vide (the Anova holds within 0.2°F), electric carving knife. Gardeners: a 6-pod AeroGarden for herbs.

Check what they already own

Ask what brand of knives or pots they use and their stove type: enameled cast iron and most sous vide sticks work on induction, gas and electric.

Food gifts are never wasted

Finishing olive oil, flaky salt and good vanilla are things cooks use up and rarely splurge on for themselves — great add-ons under $50.

Questions people ask

What do you get someone who loves to cook?

A Le Creuset Dutch oven is the classic splurge, while a wireless meat thermometer like the CHEF iQ Sense (about $48) is the most useful gift under $50. Both upgrade dishes they already make instead of adding clutter.

What are good cooking gifts under $50?

The CHEF iQ Sense thermometer, Escali Primo scale (about $25), Hamilton Beach Electric Knife Set (about $44), Graza Sizzle & Drizzle olive oils (about $40), Nielsen-Massey vanilla bean paste and Maldon sea salt all usually cost under $50.

What do you buy a serious home cook who has everything?

Pick something they wouldn't buy themselves: an indoor herb garden like the AeroGarden Harvest, a sous vide stick like the Anova Precision Cooker 3.0, or premium pantry items such as finishing olive oil and vanilla bean paste.

Is a Le Creuset Dutch oven worth it as a gift?

Yes, for a cook who braises, bakes bread or makes soups. It is enameled cast iron that works on every cooktop, needs no seasoning and is made to last for decades, which is why gift guides call it the splurge pick.

Is a sous vide cooker a good gift for a home cook?

Yes, for someone who likes gadgets and perfectly cooked meat. The Anova Precision Cooker 3.0 clips onto a pot, holds water within 0.2°F and is controlled from a phone app; most food still needs a quick sear after.

What is the best gift for someone who likes to bake?

A digital scale like the Escali Primo (weighs up to 11 lb in grams or ounces) is the biggest upgrade for bakers, and vanilla bean paste makes a great add-on.
