How to choose

Match the gift to how they brew

Pour-over fans get the most from a gooseneck kettle or a Chemex; latte lovers from an espresso machine with automatic milk like the Bambino Plus; travelers from an AeroPress.

A burr grinder is the biggest upgrade

Freshly ground beans taste noticeably better. A burr grinder like the Baratza Encore (40 settings) gives a more even grind than a blade grinder.

Keep beans fresh

Air, light and moisture make coffee go stale. A vacuum canister such as the Fellow Atmos 0.7 L holds about an 11-ounce bag.

Check sizes and what they already own

The Ember Mug 3 comes in 10 oz and 14 oz; ask yourself if they drink pods, drip or espresso before buying a machine.

Questions people ask

What do you get a coffee lover who has everything?

A smart mug like the Ember Mug 3 is a safe bet — most coffee lovers don't own one, and it keeps every cup at their favorite temperature. A vacuum canister or a gooseneck kettle are other upgrades people rarely buy for themselves.

What are good coffee gifts under $50?

The Fellow Atmos vacuum canister, AeroPress Original, Chemex pour-over, Takeya cold brew maker and Frank Body coffee scrub all usually cost under $50.

Is the Ember Mug worth it as a gift?

Yes, for anyone who lets coffee go cold. The Ember Mug 3 holds drinks at 125°F to 145°F for about 90 minutes per charge (10 oz) or all day on its coaster, and launched at $129.95 (10 oz) and $149.95 (14 oz).

What is the best coffee grinder to give as a gift?

The Baratza Encore is the go-to gift grinder: a burr grinder with 40 settings that Baratza says lasts around 10 years with care. For espresso, choose the Encore ESP version.

What's a good gift for someone who wants to make espresso at home?

The Breville Bambino Plus. It heats up in about three seconds and froths milk automatically, so beginners can make lattes and cappuccinos with simple buttons.

Is a coffee subscription a good gift?

Yes — a subscription brings fresh beans from different roasters every month. Good Morning America lists Trade Coffee plans at roughly $17 to $22 per delivery; they're bought from the roaster, not Amazon.
