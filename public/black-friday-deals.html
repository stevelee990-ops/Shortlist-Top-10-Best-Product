How to choose

Check the price history, not just the percent off

Amazon product pages can show up to 365 days of price history, according to Amazon. dealnews warns that percent-off labels are sometimes measured against an inflated list price, so a real deal should beat recent prices too.

Match the exact size and model

Deal prices usually apply to one version: the LG C6 price here is for the 65-inch set, the Apple Watch Series 12 deal is the 42mm GPS model and the Apple Watch SE 3 deal is the 40mm GPS model. Other sizes, colors and cellular versions cost more.

Know Amazon's Black Friday dates

Amazon's Black Friday Week runs from 12:01 a.m. PT on November 19 through Cyber Monday, November 30, 2026. Several prices on this page, like AirPods Pro 3 at $179, were already live before it started.

Buy gifts early and use the holiday return window

Amazon items bought November 1 to December 31, 2026 can be returned until January 31, 2027. Apple-brand products must be returned by January 15, 2027.

Prioritize electronics; wait on toys and fitness gear

dealnews names high-ticket electronics and Apple products like AirPods among the strongest Black Friday categories, while toys can get cheaper after the holidays and fitness equipment deals peak in January and February.

Questions people ask

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27. Amazon's Black Friday Week starts at 12:01 a.m. PT on Thursday, November 19 and runs through Cyber Monday, November 30, 2026.

What are the best Black Friday deals right now?

On top-rated products, the best deals we found on November 18 are the Sony WH-1000XM6 at $378 (was $459.99), Apple AirPods Pro 3 at $179 (was $249) and the 65-inch LG C6 OLED at $1,399.99 (was $2,699.99), all on Amazon. Prices change fast, so check before you buy.

Do you need Prime for Amazon Black Friday deals?

No. You don't need a Prime membership to shop Amazon's Black Friday sale, according to Yahoo Shopping. Prime costs $14.99 a month or $139 a year and adds faster delivery, per Amazon.

Are early Black Friday deals worth it?

Often, yes, for electronics: dealnews names high-ticket electronics and Apple products such as AirPods among the strongest Black Friday categories. But a sale isn't always the year's lowest price, so compare with Amazon's price history before you buy.

Is Cyber Monday better than Black Friday?

Not necessarily. dealnews says Cyber Monday and the days after it can be as good as or better than Black Friday, especially online, so missing a Black Friday price doesn't mean the deal is gone for good.

Can you return Amazon Black Friday purchases after Christmas?

Yes. Most items bought on Amazon between November 1 and December 31, 2026 can be returned until January 31, 2027. Apple-brand products have an earlier deadline of January 15, 2027.
