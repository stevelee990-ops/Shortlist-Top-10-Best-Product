How to choose

Plan on real-world runtime, not the box number

Vacuum Wars says cordless floor washers often run 20–30 minutes, and heated or high-power modes drain them faster: the Roborock F25 Ultra is rated for 60 minutes but lasted about 15–25 in heated modes. TechRadar warns a 40-minute machine may struggle in a larger home. For big homes, look for 60+ minutes on low (Tineco S9 Artist Steam, Narwal S20 Pro, Dreame Aero Pro) or a corded model like the Bissell CrossWave HydroSteam Plus.

Balance tank size against weight

Clean-water tanks here range from about 0.8 to 1 L. Bigger tanks cover more floor between refills but add weight, so Vacuum Wars suggests judging the weight with full tanks. For stairs or a small home, a 9–11 lb model (Tineco i5 Stretch, Narwal S20 Pro, Roborock F25 ACE Pro) is easier to handle than a 13–15 lb one.

Pay for hot-air drying if odors bother you

A dock that washes the roller with hot water and dries it with hot air helps limit odors and bacteria, per Vacuum Wars, which counts a missing heated dry as a con. You still need to empty and rinse the dirty-water tank, ideally after every use, per TechRadar.

Stick to sealed hard floors

These machines are made for sealed wood, laminate, vinyl, tile and stone, not unsealed wood or stone, per Expert Reviews, and steam modes are for sealed floors only, per Vacuum Wars. Most aren't meant for carpet or rugs; the Bissell CrossWave HydroSteam Plus is the exception for area rugs.

Use the cleaning solution the maker recommends

Vacuum Wars tests each model with its own recommended solution. The Shark AquaReach Premium is designed for Shark's solution, while the Dyson WashG1 works with water alone, per Reader's Digest. Bissell warns that adding vinegar to its floor cleaners can permanently damage them.

Check the head: edges, lay-flat and height

Two- or three-sided edge cleaning gets closer to baseboards (the Tineco S9 Artist Steam cleans on three sides). A 180° lay-flat handle and a low head, like the Roborock F25 Ultra's 4.9-inch profile, reach under beds and sofas.

Questions people ask

What is the best wet-dry vacuum in 2026?

The Tineco Floor One S7 Pro is the best wet-dry vacuum in 2026 for most people: it's TechRadar's best overall pick, Reader's Digest's pick for smart tech and Homes & Gardens' best for hardwood. Vacuum Wars' top pick is the Tineco Floor One S9 Artist Steam, and its best budget pick is the Tineco Floor One i5 Stretch.

Are wet-dry vacuums worth it?

Wet-dry vacuums are worth it if you have mostly hard floors and want to vacuum and mop in one pass: Reader's Digest says they're worthwhile for the convenience alone, though a traditional mop may do better on tile grout. Vacuum Wars says they're best for regular upkeep and light-to-moderate stains, not big spills or deep rug cleaning.

Can you use a wet-dry vacuum on carpet?

Most wet-dry vacuums shouldn't be used on carpet: they're built for sealed hard floors, and Vacuum Wars says using one on a small rug is technically possible but not recommended because it can leave moisture behind. Bissell's CrossWave models are the exception; Reader's Digest names the CrossWave HydroSteam Plus its best multi-surface pick for hard floors and area rugs.

Can you put vinegar in a wet-dry vacuum?

No. Bissell warns not to add vinegar to its floor cleaning machines because it can cause permanent damage. Use the solution your model's maker recommends, or plain water for machines like the Dyson WashG1, which is designed to clean with water alone.

Do you need to vacuum before using a wet-dry vacuum?

Usually not, since most wet-dry vacuums pick up dry debris and wash the floor in the same pass. The exception here is the Dyson WashG1: Reader's Digest says it doesn't work as a standalone dry vacuum, so vacuuming first is typically necessary.

How do you clean a wet-dry vacuum?

Empty and rinse the dirty-water tank after every use if you can, and clean the brush roll now and then, per TechRadar. Most models here have a self-cleaning dock that rinses the roller; better ones, like the Tineco S9 Artist Steam and Roborock F25 Ultra, also dry it with hot air to limit odors.
