How to choose

Match your phone

AirPods work best with iPhone, Galaxy Buds with Samsung and Pixel Buds with Pixel. Sony, Bose and Soundcore work well with anything.

Do you need noise cancelling?

ANC is worth it for commuting, flying and open offices. For workouts or casual listening, you can save money without it.

Fit is everything

Good sound and noise cancelling depend on a good seal. Try the different ear-tip sizes in the box.

Check battery life

Look at hours per charge (with ANC on) and total hours with the case. 6–8 hours per charge is typical.

Questions people ask

What are the best wireless earbuds overall?

The Sony WF-1000XM6 are the top pick at several major review sites for their sound and noise cancelling.

Are AirPods worth it on Android?

They work, but you lose many features. Android users are better off with Sony, Bose, Samsung or Google earbuds.

How much should I spend?

Excellent earbuds start around $60–$130. Flagships at $250–$330 add the best noise cancelling and extras.
