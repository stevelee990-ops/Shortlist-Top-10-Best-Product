How to choose

Match the bar to your TV size

A bar should be roughly as wide as your TV or a little narrower. Tom's Guide pairs 27–30-inch bars like the Bose Smart Soundbar and Sonos Beam Ultra with TVs around 55 inches and smaller, and longer bars like the 46-inch Samsung HW-QS750F or the roughly 4-foot Sonos Arc Ultra with 55-inch-plus TVs.

Read the channel numbers

In 5.1.2, the first number is the main speaker channels, the second is the subwoofer and the third is the up-firing height channels used for Dolby Atmos. A 2.0 bar like the Sony HT-S100F has no sub or height channels; an 11.1.4 system like the Samsung HW-Q990H adds rear speakers and four height channels.

Connect with HDMI eARC

Plugging into your TV's HDMI ARC or eARC port lets your TV remote control the soundbar's volume and carries Dolby Atmos from the TV's apps. If you're short on TV ports, look for HDMI inputs (passthrough): the Samsung HW-Q990H has two HDMI 2.1 inputs, while the Sonos Arc Ultra and Beam Ultra have none.

Real vs. virtual Atmos

Bars with up-firing drivers (Sonos Beam Ultra, Bose Smart Soundbar, Samsung 3.1.2 and 5.1.2 models) bounce height effects off the ceiling. Virtual Atmos bars like the Sony HT-S2000 process the effect without up-firing speakers, which costs less but sounds less overhead.

Decide on a subwoofer now or later

For TV shows and news, clear dialogue matters most and a subwoofer isn't needed. For movies, a sub adds weight: the Samsung HW-Q600F and HW-QS750F include one, while Sony's Bar 7 can add a Sub 7 later for $329 and Sonos bars pair with the Sonos Sub 4.

Keep it simple if you want to

Wi-Fi and apps add streaming and room calibration but more setup. The Sony HT-S100F, Sony HT-S2000 and Samsung HW-Q600F work without Wi-Fi; Sonos bars need the Sonos app for setup.

Questions people ask

What is the best soundbar in 2026?

The Sonos Arc Ultra is the best soundbar in 2026 for most people: What Hi-Fi? and Tom's Guide both rank it best overall for its precise Dolby Atmos, clear dialogue and punchy bass from a single bar ($1,099). For a full surround system in one box, the Samsung HW-Q990H includes a wireless subwoofer and two rear speakers.

Is a soundbar worth it?

Yes, a soundbar is worth it for most flat-screen TVs because their thin built-in speakers make dialogue hard to hear. Even the roughly $98 Sony HT-S100F is a clear step up from TV speakers according to Tom's Guide and What Hi-Fi?, and mid-range bars like the Samsung HW-Q600F add a subwoofer and Dolby Atmos.

Do I need a subwoofer with a soundbar?

No, you don't need a subwoofer for everyday TV: Tom's Guide says clear dialogue matters most for TV shows and a sub and surrounds aren't needed. For movies, a subwoofer adds much more bass; the Samsung HW-Q600F and HW-QS750F include one, and the Sony HT-S2000 has built-in woofers if you don't want a separate box.

What size soundbar do I need for a 65-inch TV?

For a 65-inch TV, choose a soundbar about 40 inches or longer, such as the 46-inch Samsung HW-QS750F or the roughly 4-foot Sonos Arc Ultra. Compact 27–30-inch bars like the Bose Smart Soundbar and Sonos Beam Ultra suit TVs around 55 inches and smaller.

What does 5.1.2 mean on a soundbar?

5.1.2 means five main speaker channels, one subwoofer channel and two up-firing height channels for Dolby Atmos. For example, the Samsung HW-QS750F is a 5.1.2 system with a wireless subwoofer and rear speakers, while the Sonos Beam Ultra is rated 7.1.2 from a single bar.

Sonos Arc Ultra vs. Sonos Beam Ultra: which should I buy?

Buy the Sonos Arc Ultra ($1,099) for a TV over 55 inches and the best single-bar sound, and the Sonos Beam Ultra ($699) for TVs around 55 inches and smaller. The Beam Ultra is about 30 inches wide with up-firing Atmos drivers; the Arc Ultra is about 4 feet long with deeper bass. Neither has HDMI passthrough or DTS:X.
